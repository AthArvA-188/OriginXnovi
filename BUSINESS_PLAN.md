## Fixed points from the build (consistent with the README and site)

- **Product:** one platform per building. That means one coordinator, one shared agent interface, and one whole-building risk picture with a ranked work queue and automated first response.
- **Expansion model:** each subsystem agent (structural visual, internal sensors, battery / fire risk, HVAC, and later plumbing and fire/smoke visual) runs on the same interface. Adding a subsystem is writing one agent, not building a new product.
- **Who it is for:** owners and facility managers of commercial buildings who today coordinate separate inspection trades on separate calendars.
- **What is proven today:** detection and prioritization logic on five real public datasets, with measured results (`data/samples/metrics.json`). The building and the mitigation actions are simulated.
